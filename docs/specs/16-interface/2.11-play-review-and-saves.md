### 2.11 Play review and Saves

**Play review** (Unplayed group; 1.1, not implemented): untouched content, 12 §3.

**Saves** (Library group, after packs) is a GUI page (`saves.js`, `view.SavesRows`, `view.SaveDetailOf`, `view.SavePlayOf`, `API.SavesRead` / `SaveDetail` / `SaveFix`). The left and middle cards come from the save heads (L0); the right card, the in-game days, the grouping by world and the consequences in Can it load? (12 §4.6) come from the full read (L1). Rules, data and actions are in 12 §1.7 and §4; the chosen draft is `docs/design/saves-drafts/b.html`. Three cards fill the window (00 §6 D20); the gaps between them drag.

- **Left: colonies and their saves.** The search is a card of its own above the tree (`/`, `esc` clears; no icon; focused, its border lights up in place, the card's size and corners): it matches colony and save names, not Mods, and expands the colonies it finds. Below it is the colonies card, with colonies by last played. A colony row shows only its name and save count; clicking it expands its saves underneath as a tree and selects the latest, and clicking the open current colony folds it. A save row shows only its name (RimSaves prefix removed, 12 §4.1) and in-game day. The left card has no status icons, descriptions or warnings.
- **Middle: the selected save.** The header has the save name (no icon) and Rename, Copy, Back up, Make a list, Recycle (12 §4.4), then only three facts: game (version and environment), list or pack, and location (folder and file, with Open location). **Can it load?** (12 §4.3) lists only what needs attention: a quick-fix bar with the verdict and at most one primary action, then the rows under **the file**, **the last load**, **game changes** and **Mod list changes**, each with its consequence and its own fix button. Passed checks are not listed. **Mod changes**: only what changed since the colony's previous save (12 §4.3), added Mods green with +, removed red with −, load order ignored; a colony's first save shows its whole list folded instead.
- **Right: the playthrough.** Colony name, day and date, storyteller, difficulty and played time (the biome is not read, 12 §1.7); stats at this save with the change since the previous save; colonists, the dead struck through; events; the timeline, whose points open their saves (12 §4.5).
- **Loading.** The left card, the middle header and the Mod list come from the save head at once (12 §1.7 L0). Consequences, play facts and grouping by world wait for the full read (L1) and show skeletons meanwhile; progress appears in the status line and in background tasks (§2.16).
- **Keys.** `/` search; ↑↓ move through visible saves; ←→ fold and unfold the current colony; `enter` runs the quick-fix bar's action; `F2` renames; `1`–`3` the colonies, the save and the playthrough, numbered as other pages number their cards (the search has no number).
- While the game runs (09 §4), Rename and Recycle are disabled with the reason. A file written in the last 5 seconds shows "being written" instead of a verdict.
- TUI: no page; the `saves` commands run through `:` (§9.1).

In detail:

- **Reading.** Opening the page shows its structure, then `API.SavesRead` lists the saves on the page's own reader in the background and sends a `saves` event (a newer request drops an older one). The reader keeps the head cache (12 §1.7), so coming back stats each save instead of reading it; leaving the page drops the listing (17 §7.3). Rescan reads again. Selecting a save runs `app.CheckSave` against its list as it is now (`API.SaveDetail`).
- **Full read** (12 §1.7 L1; `gui/saves_read.go`). After a listing that has saves not read yet, the background task "read the saves" (kind `saves`, RimArcho queue, one run at a time, stoppable) runs `archo saves index --progress <selected save>` as a child process; closing the window ends it, and each save it read stays stored. Its progress (`n/total` and the save) goes to the task record and, while the page is open, to the status line unless that shows a failure or an undo. The page hears of the run through `saves-read` events (§2.16.1 G20): the saves read, the progress, and, at most once a second and at the end, the listing again, so rows get their in-game day and colonies regroup by world while the selection and the open colonies stay; when the selected save is among those read, its cards are asked for again. A selected save that is not read yet never waits for the run: `SaveDetail` answers at once with skeletons in the right card and reads that one save on its own read session in a goroutine (one at a time; a newer selection replaces one that waits), then sends `saves-read` with it. The run may read the same save again, which only rewrites its cache row. The page keeps the facts of the selected save and of its colony's previous read save only (dropped on leaving); listings carry only summaries.
- **Left.** A save row shows its in-game day once it is read, with the date it was saved as its hover text; until then it shows the date. Search matches colony and save names on the page, marks the matches and opens the colonies found; `esc` clears it.
- **Can it load?** The heading names what the save is checked against: "against the current list X" when the save's list is its environment's current list, else "against list X" (or the pack, or the installed Mods). The bar's verdict drops "loads, but" (warnings read **content will be lost**) and says when what the Mods own could not be checked ("assemblies not analysed yet", or "Defs and assemblies" when the Defs were not either, 12 §4.6). Its one action: Recycle… for a file that cannot load; while something could not be checked, **Make a list from this save to analyse its assemblies** (`API.SaveNewList` with analyze: the list is made as below, then analysed as a background task while the page stays, and the save is checked again when the `analyze` event says it is done; the analysis indexes the save's own Mods, which is all the check needs); else Launch, or Make a list from this save for a save matching no list. There is no Fix and launch: each row has its fix. Rows by group, in this order: **the file** (unreadable, cut off, malformed), **the last load** ("the last load logged N errors and M warnings", omitting zero counts and using singular nouns for one, with when, the first message and how many kinds (12 §4.3); Open the game log selects that cluster on the game log page), **game changes** ("the game version changed since this save (1.6… → 1.7…)"), **Mod list changes**. Each row has a sign (− missing, + added, ↕ order), its heading, its consequence and its fixes. The Mods that are not installed are **one row** naming them (6 at most, then how many more), whose consequence is what they lose together (`SaveCheck.GoneOwns`: what nothing installed provides is counted once, not once per Mod) and says when the database has no data on them; its fix subscribes and enables the ones with a Workshop page; **View** opens under it each Mod with its packageId and Workshop page (or that it has none) and what is lost by kind: colonists by race, factions, classes and Defs by type, the most used 100 names of each. An installed Mod not in the list is a row headed by its name (the hover text says "installed but not in the list") with **Enable**; past 4 such rows the rest fold into one row with one fix for all of them. The Mods added since the save are one row naming them (8, then how many more) with **Disable**; the reordered ones one row with Use the save's order. A cut-off save gets Recycle… and opens the colony's previous readable save.
- **Fixes.** Every Mod fix is `API.SaveFix`: one undoable edit to the matched list, which opens in the editor without becoming current (07 §1.3); the status line's undo undoes it and checks the save again. Workshop Mods added by a fix are then subscribed through the Steam / SteamCMD choice that missing Mods use. A pack's save, or one matching no list, offers no Mod fix. `archo saves fix --launch` (12 §4.4) fixes and also launches; the page does not offer that.
- **Mod changes** (`SaveDetail.Since`, `Changes`). The heading names the previous save as a link that selects it; under it the counts ("3 added", "1 removed"), then one 24 px row per change: the sign, the name, "not installed" on an added Mod that is not. A name whose Mod is installed (`Library`) links to it in the Mod library (its packageId and "show in the mod library" as hover text). Past 12 rows the rest fold behind Show all N; Show fewer folds them again; the choice holds while the save stays selected. No change says "the same Mods as the previous save". A colony's first save (no previous) shows "first save of this colony: N Mods" with the whole list in load order folded behind Show all (rows numbered, marked as before: not installed, not in the list, ↕). Only the rows in view and 8 on each side are mounted as the card scrolls (§11 A18), each a list item with its position. Make a list (header) takes the path of All lists' "from a save" (`view.NewPlan`), names the list after the colony, and opens it. Selecting a save from Can it run's last save (§2.4) opens the page on it (`save:<ref>`).
- **Files.** Rename (`F2` or double-click the name) edits the name in place; a taken name is refused while typing; `enter` or leaving the field saves, `esc` cancels. Copy selects the copy. Back up and Recycle show an inline confirmation in the middle card listing the files and sizes and where copies go; `esc` cancels. While the game runs, Rename and Recycle are disabled with the reason.
- **Right** (`view.SavePlayOf`, the draft's right column). The header has the colony name, the in-game day and date (the game's quadrum names; the date may be a day off, 12 §1.7), the storyteller and difficulty by their in-game names for the vanilla Defs (a Mod's by Def name; commitment mode added) and the real play time. **At this save**: wealth (the game's `Wealth_Total` record; left out when the save has none), colonists alive, animals, research points (all projects' points: whether one is finished needs its Def, 12 §1.7 L2), raids, and prisoners and mechanoids when either save has any, each with its change since the colony's previous read save, which the heading names (no change without one); then the colonists, the living first and the dead struck through, at most 40 with a quarter of the places kept for the dead and the rest counted. **Events**: the newest 8 letters with their day. **Over time**: this save's own wealth and colonist records from day 0 to its day (thinned to 120 values each), a mark per death letter, and one point per read save of the colony at its day, on the wealth line where the line reaches it and on the axis after it; a point opens its save, and the chart reads as one image (the tree reaches every save by keys). A malformed save shows what was read before the break, with a note; one that breaks off before the game's time shows only where it breaks off. While the save is read the card shows skeletons; a save being written or that could not be read says so; no number is made up.
- **Keys.** Besides the keys above, `1`–`3` focus the cards, each with its number before its heading. Saves has no `shift+` number: `shift+1`–`8` stay on the other eight entries (§3.5).
- **Size.** The page's accessibility tree and mounted rows follow §11 A18 and the targets in §10; measured: 18 §2 (Saves page full read) and §11 A18.
