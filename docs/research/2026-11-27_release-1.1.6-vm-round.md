## Bugs

1. **First-run guide: the "started directly" chip keeps the old language after a language pick** (fixed, 54fc4910). Severity: low. Steps: fresh home, the guide's language row, pick `日本語`. Actual: the page reads Japanese but the 1.7 card's chip says `直接启动`. Cause: `GameFound.Source` is translated by detection (`view.gameSource`) and the guide redrew from the cards it had. Fix: after a pick the guide reads the cards again (`api.GuideFirstRun`) and takes their chips, keeping every pick; test `found: a language pick rereads the game cards' chips and keeps the picks`. Checked in the VM after the fix: `Steam | 直接起動しました`, also after a restart.
