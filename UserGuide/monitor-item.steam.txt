The in-game companion Mod of the RimArcho launcher. It records the game's errors and loading stages, and points each error at the Mod likely behind it.
[list]
[*]Changes nothing in the game.
[*]Add or remove it at any time.
[*]Next to no performance cost.
[/list]
To see what it found, get RimArcho (free, MIT). RimArcho was taken down by community moderators the last time it was on the Workshop, so only download links are given here (see Download).

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ToumanLin/RimArcho/master/docs/assets/workshop-monitor/en/header-02-rimarcho.png[/img]
A clean, fast, lightweight RimWorld launcher and Mod manager. [strike]It has the cutest Archo-chan.[/strike]
[list]
[*][b]Light:[/b] core 47 MB of memory, peak <250 MB, ~100 MB at rest (RimSort and RimCrow: 500+ MB; 443-Mod list, 5,143 Mods installed).
[*][b]Sort:[/b] built from 40,326 player error logs (26 GB) and 5,143 Mods. I believe it's the best in the community today.
[*][b]Errors and conflicts:[/b] error patterns and severity counted from those logs (most red text needs no worry); Mods that often conflict in them are flagged.
[*][b]With Archo Monitor:[/b] in-game information the outside can't see, so errors are easier to locate and debug.
[*][b]Saves:[/b] which Mods a save needs, and whether removing or adding one breaks it, loses content or does nothing.
[*][b]Mod copies:[/b] make a copy and switch to it in one click; Steam won't update it.
[*][b]Game versions:[/b] several versions and configs side by side (yes, 1.6 and 1.7 at once), download versions with SteamCMD, back up the current one (Tynan-flare protection, lol).
[*][b]Subscriptions:[/b] GitHub and LoversLab, checked for updates.
[*][b]Bring your data:[/b] import from RimSort, RimCrow and RimPy.
[*][b]Archo MCP:[/b] 47 tools, from the right Mod folder and a precise error log to launching and testing the game.
[*][b]RimWorld Probe skill:[/b] your agent launches the game, loads a save, plays and takes screenshots. No need to describe why your prisoner can't path-find: it sees it.
[/list]

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ToumanLin/RimArcho/master/docs/assets/workshop-monitor/en/header-03-ai.png[/img]
Say "something's wrong": your agent looks into it, fixes it, launches the game to check the fix, and tells you.
I built test cases from real player logs and real Mods and ran them in a VM. Pi Agent (DeepSeek 4.1 Flash) with Archo MCP vs. without: 67% → 100% correct, up to 50% fewer tokens.
