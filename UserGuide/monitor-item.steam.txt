Archo Monitor is the in-game companion Mod of the RimArcho launcher. It records the game's errors and loading stages, and points each error at the Mod likely behind it.
[list]
[*]Changes nothing in the game.
[*]Add or remove it at any time.
[*]Next to no performance cost.
[/list]
To see what it found, get RimArcho (free, MIT). The last time RimArcho was published on the Workshop it was taken down by community moderators, so only download links are given here (see below).

A RimWorld launcher and Mod manager that looks past your current list: game versions, playthroughs and saves, one click apart. When something breaks, your AI agent launches the game and finds the culprit: no more halving your Mod list for hours. [strike]RimArcho has the cutest Archo-chan.[/strike]
[list]
[*][b]Platforms and languages:[/b] Windows (stable) and Linux (testing); English, Chinese and Japanese.
[*][b]Light:[/b] core 47 MB of memory, peak <250 MB, ~100 MB at rest (RimSort and RimCrow: usually 500+ MB; tested with my 450-Mod list).
[*][b]Bring your data:[/b] import from RimSort, RimCrow and RimPy: Mod lists, aliases, notes, tags, colour marks, ignored warnings and sorting rules; nothing already there is imported twice.
[*][b]Sorting:[/b] built from 40,326 player error logs (26 GB, updated weekly) and 5,143 Mods. I believe RimArcho's sort is the best in the community today.
[*][b]Errors and conflicts:[/b] from those 40,326 logs I counted error patterns and rated their severity (and found most red text needs no worry); I also found Mods that often conflict, and RimArcho warns you if you enable them together.
[*][b]Archo Monitor:[/b] in-game information the outside can't see, so errors are easier to locate and debug.
[*][b]Saves:[/b] which Mods a save needs, and whether removing or adding one breaks it, loses content or does nothing.
[*][b]Mod copies:[/b] make a copy and switch to it in one click; Steam won't update it, so an update can't bring new errors.
[*][b]Game versions:[/b] several game versions and configs side by side (yes, 1.6 and 1.7 at once), download versions with SteamCMD, back up the current game version in one click (Tynan-flare protection, lol).
[*][b]Subscriptions:[/b] GitHub and LoversLab, checked for updates automatically.
[/list]

RimArcho also comes with Archo MCP and the RimWorld Probe skill:
[list]
[*][b]Archo MCP:[/b] 47 MCP tools, from the right Mod folder, to a precise error log, to launching the game and testing it: a complete RimWorld debugging kit.
[*][b]RimWorld Probe skill:[/b] a probe Mod skeleton that lets your AI agent build its own Mod to control the game, so it can test the game itself. You no longer have to work out how to describe in words when and where your prisoner can't path-find.
[/list]
Archo MCP's goal: you say "something's wrong", and your agent looks into it, fixes it, launches the game to check the fix, and tells you.
I built test cases from real player logs and real Mods and ran them in a VM. Pi Agent (DeepSeek 4.1 Flash) with Archo MCP vs. without: 67% → 100% correct, up to 50% fewer tokens.
